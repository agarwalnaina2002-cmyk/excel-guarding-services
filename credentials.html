What we say we do for every guard, and the records you can ask to see before you sign.

01For every guard

Statutory duties, on record.

Each practice below sits beside the evidence that backs it. Ask for any of it during a site assessment or before you sign.

PracticeWhat it meansEvidence
  1. 01

    Wages paid on time

    Guards’ wages are paid on time, every month.

    Evidence to supply: payroll date and payment method
  2. 02

    PF and ESI

    Provident Fund and Employees’ State Insurance contributions are deducted, paid and filed for every guard.

    Evidence to supply: PF and ESI registration codes and sample challans
  3. 03

    GST

    GST is charged on our invoices and our returns are filed.

    Evidence to supply: GSTIN
  4. 04

    Guard verification

    Every guard is identified through Aadhaar and verified by a third party before deployment.

    Evidence to supply: verification agency and the record a client can see (never Aadhaar numbers)
  5. 05

    Labour Department

    We liaise with the Labour Department on the obligations that apply to our guards.

    Evidence to supply: contract labour licence or registration, if applicable
  6. 06

    Local police

    We liaise with the local police where our guards are deployed.

    Evidence to supply: what this liaison involves in practice
