The brief

Where guarding fails

Most security failures are quiet. A post left empty during a handover. A visitor waved through because the badge looked right. An incident written up the next afternoon. We build guarding around those moments.

Handover

No post is left while one officer hands over to the next. The handover is logged.

Visitors

Everyone who comes in goes through the access rules written for your site.

Reporting

Incidents are recorded during the shift they happen and escalated to the people you name.

02How the work is held together

Three things decide whether a guard force works. We manage all three.

  1. 01

    People

    Guards are chosen for temperament as well as availability, and trained at our own academy. Nobody works your site until they have been trained on its policies and procedures.

    • A specialised selection process
    • Training on your site’s policies before the first shift
    • The same core team on your site wherever possible Confirm this commitment
  2. 02

    Process

    Every position has written post instructions: what to check, who may enter, when to patrol, and who to call when something goes wrong. Officers work to the document, not to memory.

    • Post instructions written for your site
    • Patrol routes and timings agreed with you
    • An escalation list with names and numbers
  3. 03

    Oversight

    Supervisors check every site, including at night. Confirm whether visits are also unannounced They check logs against what actually happened on the ground, and you get a performance report every month, not a verbal assurance.

    • Supervision at several levels, with night checks
    • Logs and patrol records checked against each other
    • A monthly guard performance report to you

05Officer pathway

An officer earns your site step by step.

  1. Step 01

    Selection

    A specialised selection process, then Aadhaar and third-party verification before training begins.

  2. Step 02

    Academy training

    At least 160 hours at EAST, our own academy, indoors and outdoors, including firefighting.

  3. Step 03

    Site familiarisation

    Training on your site’s own policies and procedures: routes, people, systems, and what normal looks like there.

  4. Step 04

    Deployment

    A named post with written instructions, under supervision at several levels.

  5. Step 05

    Review & refresh

    Monthly performance checks, including night checks, feed back into periodic training.

Courses follow the Security Sector Skill Development Council syllabus for guards and supervisors. Licensing records: confirm what we can show for guards on your site
