The brief

Where guarding fails

Most security failures are quiet. A post left empty during a handover. A visitor waved through because the badge looked right. An incident written up the next afternoon. We build guarding around those moments.

Handover

No post is left while one officer hands over to the next. The handover is logged.

Visitors

Everyone who comes in goes through the access rules written for your site.

Reporting

Incidents are recorded during the shift they happen and escalated to the people you name.

02How the work is held together

Three things decide whether a guard force works. We manage all three.

  1. 01

    People

    Officers are chosen for temperament as well as availability. Nobody works your site alone until they have been inducted on it and shadowed an experienced officer there.

    • Screening and interview before an offer
    • A site induction before the first solo shift
    • The same core team on your site wherever possible
  2. 02

    Process

    Every position has written post instructions: what to check, who may enter, when to patrol, and who to call when something goes wrong. Officers work to the document, not to memory.

    • Post instructions written for your site
    • Patrol routes and timings agreed with you
    • An escalation list with names and numbers
  3. 03

    Oversight

    Supervisors visit, including unannounced. They check logs against what actually happened on the ground, and you get the findings in writing, not a verbal assurance.

    • Supervisor visits, scheduled and unannounced
    • Logs and patrol records checked against each other
    • Written reports and a regular review with you

04Officer pathway

An officer earns your site step by step.

  1. Step 01

    Selection

    Screening, reference and background checks as the law requires, then an interview focused on judgement.

  2. Step 02

    Core induction

    Conduct, reporting standards, emergency procedures and how we expect officers to deal with the public.

  3. Step 03

    Site familiarisation

    Shadow shifts on your site: routes, people, systems, and what normal looks like there.

  4. Step 04

    Deployment

    A named post with written instructions, and a supervisor checking in during the first weeks.

  5. Step 05

    Review & refresh

    Supervisor observations and incident reviews feed back into refresher training.

Licensing and mandatory training follow the regulations where you operate. We’ll show you the records for the officers on your site.

Due diligence

Don’t take a security company’s word for it. Ask for the documents.

Before you sign, we’ll give you everything on this list. If a provider can’t produce these, treat that as an answer.

Request the document pack
  1. DOC 01

    Company registration and operating licence

    Our legal entity and the licence to provide security services where your site is.

  2. DOC 02

    Insurance certificates

    Current public and employer’s liability cover, with policy limits stated.

  3. DOC 03

    Officer licensing and training records

    For each officer we propose for your site, as far as privacy law allows.

  4. DOC 04

    Sample post instructions and shift report

    Real formats, with client details removed, so you can see what you would receive.

  5. DOC 05

    Client references

    Contacts at sites similar to yours, shared with their permission.
