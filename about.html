A guarding company that trains its own guards.

03Why EGS

What we do differently, from hiring to night checks.

A

Selection & training

  • A specialised selection process to find the right people
  • Our own training academy, EAST
  • Assessment before and after training
  • Periodic, industry-specific and on-site training
  • Training on each site’s own policies and procedures
  • A defined career path for guards
B

Supervision

  • Guard performance checked on site, including night checks
  • A performance report to the client every month
  • Security audits of guards on site, with reports to the client
  • Supervision at several levels
  • A 24/7 control room
C

Your side of the contract

  • A predefined escalation matrix, so you know who to call
  • Regular feedback from clients
  • Statutory compliance handled for every guard, with evidence
