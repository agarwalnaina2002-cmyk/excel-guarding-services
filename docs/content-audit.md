### Implemented in this pass
- **Services page:** new meta description listing the real services. One H1, H2 per section, H3/H4 for groups and services. Descriptive anchors replace "Read more".
- **Navigation:** crawlable links to all 12 service anchors from every page.
- **Structured data:**
  - `WebSite` (home)
  - `BreadcrumbList` on every inner page
  - `ItemList` of 12 `Service` items, each linked to the business by `@id` (services page)
  - `areaServed` deliberately omitted until coverage is confirmed
  - Existing `LocalBusiness` and `EducationalOrganization` kept
- **Mobile:** "Open in Google Maps" link on Contact.
- **Deploy config:** `.vercelignore` keeps `docs/`, `README.md` and `server.js` off the public site.

### Already in place (checked)
- **Pages:** unique titles and descriptions, a canonical on every page, and Open Graph tags.
- **Indexing:** `sitemap.xml` (8 URLs; gallery and privacy are `noindex`) and `robots.txt` with a Sitemap line.
- **URLs:** clean URLs, and 308 redirects from the old site's page names.

### Needs external validation
- Google Rich Results Test and Search Console, once the final domain is chosen.
- Google Business Profile: link it if one exists, and make sure its name, address and phone match.
- Moving the canonical domain to excelguardingservices.com, if that's the plan.

## 4. Discrepancies and risks for EGS to resolve
1. "Nearly three decades" (company) vs "30+ years" (MD personally). Need the founding year.
2. MD shown as "Managing Director" and "Chairman" (the latter appears to be his APSA role).
3. "APSA" could mean two different listed bodies. IISSM and CAPSI roles conflict between the Home and Management pages.
4. Academy: "160 hours (20 days)" vs 14 + 7 = 21 days. "Affiliated by PMKY" is unclear.
5. Cash van services appear on Home but not on What we do. Cash-in-transit carries its own regulatory requirements.
6. Jammers are listed twice. They're restricted in India and not published.
7. Phone "+91-96405 66695 / 96" read as two numbers. The 040 landlines may be out of service.
8. Academy PIN is truncated ("500 03"). The map pin is named "Excel Security Services".
9. 2021 copyright on the original. Any detail may be out of date.
10. Gmail address for a corporate brand. Consider a company-domain email.
