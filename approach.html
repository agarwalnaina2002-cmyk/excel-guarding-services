Approach

Guarding is a management job. We run it like one.

What happens behind the officer you see: who they are, what they work to, who checks them, and who you call.

01People

Selection and training

An officer’s first shift on your site should not be their first time seeing it. Each stage below must be completed before the next.

  1. 01

    Selection

    A specialised selection process, with Aadhaar and third-party verification of every guard, then an interview that tests judgement, not just availability.

  2. 02

    Academy training

    A minimum of 160 hours at EAST, our own academy, to the Security Sector Skill Development Council syllabus, with an assessment after each phase.

  3. 03

    Licensing

    Deployed under our licence as a private security agency in Telangana. Confirm PSARA licence details and any guard-level requirements

  4. 04

    Site familiarisation

    Training on your site’s own policies and procedures: routes, systems, key contacts, and what normal looks like there. Confirm whether this includes shadow shifts

  5. 05

    Review & refresh

    Monthly performance checks, incident reviews and site changes feed into periodic training.

02Process

Every post has a written brief.

Post instructions are written for your site after the assessment, and you approve them before deployment. They are kept at the post and updated whenever the site changes.

When an officer is unsure, the answer should be in the document. If it isn’t, the document is updated.

03Oversight

Checked on the ground, reported on paper.

Supervision runs at several levels, backed by a 24/7 control room. The frequency of each check depends on the size and risk of the site.

Every shift

Handover

The incoming officer reads the log and is briefed in person. Nothing open is left unexplained.

Day & night

Checks

Supervisors check presence, conduct, logs and patrol records against the post instructions. Confirm whether checks are also unannounced

Every incident

Reports

Written up during the shift, escalated to your named contacts, and followed up until it is closed.

Every month

Report

A guard performance report sent to you, and regular feedback from you on how the site is run.

Escalation

When something goes wrong, everyone knows who calls whom.

A predefined escalation matrix, agreed with you before deployment. Confirm these four levels match EGS’s matrix

  1. 01

    Officer on post

    Makes the scene safe, calls emergency services if needed, records what happened.

  2. 02

    Site supervisor

    Attends or directs, decides whether to escalate further, starts the incident report.

  3. 03

    Operations manager

    Owns the response for serious incidents and arranges extra cover if needed.

  4. 04

    Your named contact

    Informed in the order and timeframe set out in your post instructions.

See it written for your site.

After a site assessment we’ll send recommended posts and draft post instructions for your review. There’s no obligation to go further.
