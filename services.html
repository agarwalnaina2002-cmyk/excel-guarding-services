02Guard posts

How guards are deployed on your site.

Most sites need a mix. We recommend posts and hours after a site assessment, not before. Confirm these five descriptions: written for this site, not taken from the original

01Static guardingOfficers at fixed posts, patrolling routes agreed with you.
Typical sites
Corporate offices, business parks, warehouses, industrial and mixed-use premises.
Covers
  • Fixed posts and internal patrols
  • Opening, lock-up and alarm setting
  • Out-of-hours and weekend presence
  • First response to alarms and incidents on site
You receive
  • Post instructions for each position
  • Daily occurrence log
  • Incident reports written up in the same shift
  • Supervisor visit records
02Reception securityThe first person visitors meet, and the last check before they are inside.
Typical sites
Head offices, multi-tenant buildings, client-facing and regulated premises.
Covers
  • Visitor sign-in and pass issue
  • Contractor and delivery control
  • Lobby presence during working hours
  • Lost property and key control
You receive
  • Access rules agreed with your facilities team
  • A visitor record kept to your data policy
  • Officers briefed on your company and its people
03Gatehouse & vehicle controlEvery vehicle and person crossing the boundary, at shift change and at 2 a.m.
Typical sites
Campuses, manufacturing plants, logistics yards and gated corporate sites.
Covers
  • Vehicle and driver checks
  • Contractor passes and permits to work
  • Barrier and gate operation
  • Material in and out checks, where required
You receive
  • Vehicle and visitor logs
  • Gate procedures for peak and out-of-hours periods
  • Exceptions reported the same day
04Mobile patrolsRecorded checks for sites that don’t need an officer all night.
Typical sites
Smaller offices, vacant or part-occupied buildings, multi-site estates.
Covers
  • Scheduled and varied-time patrols
  • Lock and unlock visits
  • Perimeter, door and window checks
You receive
  • A record of every visit and what was found
  • Issues raised the same day
  • A patrol schedule you can adjust
05Events & temporary coverExtra officers for a set period, briefed before they arrive.
Typical needs
Corporate events, refurbishments, shutdowns, relocations and holiday periods.
Covers
  • Access and guest-list control
  • Additional posts for the duration
  • Cover while your regular team is stretched
You receive
  • A short plan agreed in advance
  • A named lead on the day
  • A written close-out report

05FAQs

Common questions.

How do we enquire about security services?

Use the enquiry form, call +91 96405 66695 or email excelguardingservices@gmail.com. Tell us about the site, the cover you have now and when you need it.

How do you work out what our site needs?

Our operations team discusses your requirements, then assesses the site. We recommend the posts and hours that fit your environment and budget.

How are your guards trained?

At our own academy, EAST in Peerzadiguda: at least 160 hours of indoor and outdoor training to the Security Sector Skill Development Council syllabus, including firefighting, with an assessment after each phase. They are then trained on your site’s policies.

How is the work supervised?

Supervision runs at several levels, backed by a 24/7 control room. Guard performance is checked on site, including at night, and reported to you every month. A predefined escalation matrix sets out who is called, and when.

Are your guards verified?

Every guard is verified through Aadhaar and a third party, and wages, PF, ESI and GST are handled for each one. Compliance & credentials

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Somajiguda, Hyderabad, Telangana. Cities and states served to confirm

Not sure what you need?

Start with the problem, not the service.

A back door that gets propped open, a reception overwhelmed at 9 a.m., a yard empty at weekends. Tell us what worries you, and we’ll recommend cover once we’ve seen the site.
